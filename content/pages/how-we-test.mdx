## 1. We sign up

We create a real account and use the paid plan, like you would. No demo accounts provided by vendors.

## 2. We use it for weeks

Real projects, real data, for at least 14 days per tool. EDIT: describe your typical test scenario for each category.

## 3. We score it

Every tool gets the same grid, out of 10:

- **Ease of use** — time to get value, learning curve, interface quality
- **Features** — depth of the features that matter for the category
- **Value for money** — real price for a team of 5, after year one
- **Support** — speed and quality of answers to 3 real questions

The overall score is the average, rounded to one decimal.

## 4. We update it

Prices and features change. We re-check every review at least every 6 months and show the last update date on each page.

## Independence

Vendors cannot pay for a review or a better score. Affiliate commissions and sponsored banners never affect rankings.
