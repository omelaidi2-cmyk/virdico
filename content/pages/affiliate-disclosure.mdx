## Affiliate links

Some links on Virdico are affiliate links. If you click one and buy, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. These links are marked with `rel="sponsored"`.

## Sponsored banners

Advertising banners are labeled **Sponsored**. Advertisers do not get any say in our reviews or rankings.

## Our promise

Commissions and advertising never change our scores, our rankings or what we write. If a tool is not good, we say so — even if it pays a commission.
